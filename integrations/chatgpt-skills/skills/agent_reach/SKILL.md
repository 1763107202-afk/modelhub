## Use when
User asks to research public web sources, videos, repositories, RSS or social platforms.

## Procedure
1. Identify the source, timeframe and information required.
2. Prefer existing authorized web/GitHub tools. Record URLs and dates and cite evidence.
3. Never imply Agent-Reach CLI is installed unless agent-reach doctor or a concrete call succeeds in the configured runtime.
4. For authenticated platforms, request approved access and keep cookies/tokens out of repository and output.
5. Do not bypass paywalls, access controls, rate limits or platform restrictions.
6. If the platform adapter is absent, report the exact limitation and offer publicly available alternatives.
